# EP2 + L1b EC retest — 2026-10-20 (both GPUs, final)

## Why retest
Oct-18 EP2 flat result (~11 agg) was measured BEFORE L1b EC tuning existed
(margin 300, 600 slots, no EC_EVERY=4). Hypothesis: doubled EC capacity +
half experts/rank (192/384) might flip EP2 above single-GPU.

## Setup
- `serve-ep2-l1b.sh`: TP2+EP (last-flag-wins override of entrypoint's TP1),
  both cards, EP patches from project-maya/dsv41-3090/patches (exl3-ep2.py
  3768L, engram_disk-ep2.py) bind-mounted over image files.
- Boot: clean, 192/192 experts per rank, quality gate 17*19=323 PASS.
- NOTE NVLink: nvidia-smi nvlink -s shows links INACTIVE on both cards;
  P2P copy test = 10.4 GB/s, peer access False. GPU0 swap (flaky FE -> new
  card) likely left the NVLink bridge off the new card. EP2 all-to-all
  therefore rides PCIe, not NV4.

## Measured (bench_decode.sh, warm)
| config | single t/s | agg @2 | agg @4 | agg @8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EP2 + L1b EC | 7.14-7.37 | 9.1 | 12.1 | 17.5 (1.6/stream) |
| L1b single-GPU (same day) | **10.9** | — | **17.2** | — |

## Verdict
EP2 confirmed dead on this rig — worse single (7.4 vs 10.9), worse aggregate
at useful concurrency (12.1 vs 17.2 @4). The L1b EC tuning does NOT rescue it.
Mechanism: per-layer all-to-all dispatch latency over PCIe (no NVLink) exceeds
the bandwidth win of halved per-rank expert bytes. @8 aggregate 17.5 slightly
beats L1b@4 17.2 but at 1.6 t/s per stream — unusable latency.
