DeepSeek Models

DeepSeek is a primary KTransformers model family for both inference and SFT. Inference and fine-tuning use different methods and package paths, so their statuses are listed separately.

Fine-Tuning

Use DeepSeek SFT. DeepSeek V4-Flash 0.7.2 completed a two-RTX-5090, 20-step LoRA train–save–load smoke with native MXFP4 routed experts. Training TPS was not measured. DeepSeek V3 public FP8 checkpoints follow separate AMXBF16, AMXINT8, or AMXINT4 preparation paths.
